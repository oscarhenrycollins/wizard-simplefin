# SimpleFIN for Wizard

A Wizard app that connects your banks through [SimpleFIN](https://beta-bridge.simplefin.org/). Link your banks in your own SimpleFIN account ($15 a year, paid to SimpleFIN), paste one setup token, and your other Wizard apps get read-only accounts, balances and transactions through the `bank-connection` spot.

Your bank passwords go to SimpleFIN, never to this app. The private access address and synced data stay in the app's own folder on your box.

Install: `wam install wizard-simplefin`
