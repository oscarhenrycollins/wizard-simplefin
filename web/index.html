Set up 4 steps

  1. SimpleFIN links to Chase, Amex, Bank of America, Capital One and thousands of other U.S. and Canadian banks. It costs $15 a year (or $1.50 a month), paid to SimpleFIN.

    On SimpleFIN's site, tap Get Started, enter your email, then open the sign-in link they email you.

    Open SimpleFIN ↗

  2. In SimpleFIN, under Financial Institutions, tap New Connection, find your bank and sign in. Repeat for each bank. SimpleFIN asks you to subscribe before the first one.

    Your bank password goes to SimpleFIN, never to this app.

  3. In SimpleFIN, under Apps, tap New Connection. Name it Wizard, tap Create Setup Token and copy the long token.

    It works once. If it gets lost, just create another.
